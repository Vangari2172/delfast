FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Don’t see yours here? Message us and you will hear back from the people who run the trucks.

Ask on WhatsApp
What kind of cargo do you handle?

Primarily retail displays and fixtures — floor stands, gondolas, lit units, kiosks and shop fittings — plus express part loads and full trucks across India.

Do you deliver outside Maharashtra?

Yes. We move cargo door to door across India by road and air from our Vasai East hub.

Can you deliver straight to a store or mall?

That is our standard. We deliver to the store, mall or site you name — not just a dock or counter.

How is the price worked out?

By size, weight, packing, pickup and drop cities, and whether you need part load, full truck, surface or air. Share the details and we will send a rate.

How long does delivery take?

Depends on the lane and mode. Surface express covers most routes in a few days; air is used when a store launch cannot wait.

Is my shipment insured?

Transit cover can be arranged for the declared value of the consignment. Ask us when you request a quote.

Can I track my shipment?

You deal directly with the family that runs the trucks, so you get status updates on the movement of your cargo.

Do you offer payment terms for businesses?

Yes. Customised payment terms can be set up around your billing cycle for regular manufacturers and brand partners.
